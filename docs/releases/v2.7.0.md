# CampusBridge v2.7.0

Release date: 2026-10-28

The approved work through Phase 4 is delivered as one release. The plan’s intermediate v2.5.2 and v2.6.0 labels were not separately published. Group A and Group B stay on their assigned subdomains; the Phase 5 merge is not part of this release.

## Group A: Maps and on-campus housing

Explore four on-campus housing locations alongside the community directory, with the map loading only on request and list controls retained when paused.

The Group A website restriction was added to the existing Google Maps key with owner approval. Coordinates use UTD’s public campus map. These listings do not establish vacancies or housing eligibility.

## Group A: Connected arrival planner

Start from shared arrival and apartment choices, review sourced airport options and an arrival-day checklist, and explicitly save a private Group A route to your account.

UTD planning figures and review dates accompany Silver Line, Big Howdy, late-arrival and carry-on guidance. Student GoPass eligibility must be checked; a pre-semester airport trip is not assumed free. Custom addresses are stored only after explicit saving consent.

## Group A: Team research

Keep expansion and premium concepts in a clearly labelled team worksheet while the work remains on Group A.

## Group B: Connected budget estimates

Compare up to three plan-connected budgets with editable per-person ranges, unknown costs, monthly totals, move-in cash and conditional Spring 2027 charges.

All 30 communities have quote fields. This is record coverage, not 30 verified price quotes: unavailable public prices remain Unknown. Published provider offers retain conditions. Whole-unit costs split only after the number of people is confirmed. UT SHIP semester billing and prepaid phone cash are separated from amortized monthly shares.

## Group B: Account-saved budgets and reminders

Choose account saving for each tool, load it on another device, handle conflicting saves, remove saved copies and explicitly import earlier browser copies.

New Group A and Group B tables use verified-session row policies and ownership checks. The site API also enforces exact assigned hosts. Account deletion cascades to the new records. Browser copies on another device or origin still require removal there.

## Group B: Spring reminders and glossary

Add sourced Spring 2027 reminders, choose dates where no exact deadline is verified, export calendar events and look up F1 and UTD terms.

Date-only or ambiguous publisher times are disclosed. The initial F1 entry window uses the I-20 program start, not January 19 classes. TB appointment opening is separate from completion requirements. Calendar apps control imported alerts; CampusBridge sends no background reminder notifications.

## Group B: Source reports

Submit an issue from an official source card and see the status of your own reports in a private account queue.

Submitting creates a private report linked to the signed-in account. Status does not imply an issue is fixed. The team targets review within three days; reports must exclude sensitive documents.

## Shared: Spring 2027 plan review

Add conditional entry, immigration check-in, orientation, health-requirement and insurance navigation to the shared plan with official sources and review dates.

Existing task IDs and progress are preserved. A student chooses whether conditional tasks apply and can mark them not applicable. The official housing move-in page still describes Fall 2026, and the embedded official campus map returned an error during review; those limitations remain visible.
