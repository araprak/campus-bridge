# CampusBridge v2.5.0

Release date: 2026-10-25

Group A receives feature sets 3, 5 and 12 from the Unified Feature Update Register. Group B receives sets 6, 8 and 9. These are incremental tools on the existing CampusBridge application. The shared version history continues from v2.4.0.

## Separate group sites

- Group A: https://grp-a.campusbridgeguide.com
- Group B: https://grp-b.campusbridgeguide.com

Each address identifies its group and opens only that group’s new tools at `/group-tools`. The main site retains the shared foundation. Existing accounts, saved plans and favorites use the same account service. Sign in separately on each subdomain; password recovery continues through the main site. Account protection and the retired public demo remain in place.

These are separate feature experiences in one maintained application, not separate student databases. Existing baseline tools remain available in both groups. No account migration is required.

## Group A: Google Maps experience

The enhanced housing explorer loads Google Maps only after an explicit map action. Searching, ZIP changes and radius changes can be used with the list alone. Pause removes the map renderer while retaining filters, property selection, comparison and nearby layer choices for the visit. The browser may retain map code already downloaded. The list, official community links and directions remain usable when the interactive map is unavailable.

The existing list/map selection, mobile view switching and curated nearby layers are retained. No Google billing, quota or API-key restrictions were changed. Google’s availability and the configured key’s domain restrictions still apply; no live pricing or inventory is added.

## Group A: Transportation and arrival

The arrival assistant builds directions from DFW or Dallas Love Field to a UTD destination, listed apartment community or entered address. Choose public transit, pickup or driving. Directions preserve the selected origin, destination and travel mode. Official airport, Comet Cruiser, DART/GoPass and UTD parking sources were checked on October 25, 2026 and remain linked for current requirements.

The tool does not promise current fares, schedules, travel times, availability or eligibility. Check the real terminal, transfers, pickup arrangements and final destination before travelling. An entered address stays in page memory; opening Google Maps sends the selected route to Google.

## Group A: Campus expansion and premium research

Compare another-campus guidance and optional planning-convenience concepts, record the problem, evidence, owner, success measure and decision date, and download a research worksheet. An evidence checklist supports a team review.

Feature set 12 is a research release, as scoped in the register. It does not launch another campus, subscriptions, payment processing or paid services. Notes stay in page memory until downloaded; keep personal student information out of research notes.

## Group B: Expenses and budgeting

Compare up to three scenarios using your own per-person amounts. Unknown amounts remain distinct from a confirmed zero. Integer-cent totals separate recurring monthly costs, nonrefundable one-time costs, refundable deposits and the cash needed for move-in. Incomplete estimates are labelled. Invalid amounts remain unknown and are flagged; totals are labelled incomplete.

Saving is optional and requires the “Save my scenarios on this browser” choice. Copies are scoped to the account, device and Group B subdomain and do not sync to the account database or another device. Delete controls remove the saved copy. Figures are user-entered planning values, not provider quotes.

## Group B: Helpful reminders

Reminders are off by default. Opt in to save personal titles and deadlines locally for the current account and subdomain. Choose a date, time and IANA time zone. Skipped or repeated daylight-saving times are rejected rather than silently moved. Due and upcoming statuses update while the tool is open.

Download an `.ics` file for a fixed-time calendar event. Each reminder has an optional, initially unchecked request for a calendar alert 15 minutes before. Importing, permissions and actual delivery depend on the calendar app. CampusBridge does not send reminder emails, texts or background push notifications. Turning reminders off clears their local copy; imported calendar events must be removed in that calendar separately.

## Group B: Accessibility and trust

A searchable twelve-term glossary explains common housing, budgeting and reminder terms. A limited Hindi/Korean pilot adds short explanations for four everyday terms. The pilot has not received professional language review and does not translate the whole site or official instructions.

Three official UTD resource cards show the publisher and October 25 review date for their specific summaries. The date does not certify all CampusBridge content or live prices and schedules. Students can prepare and download an outdated-information report, or open an email draft when a support address is configured. A local draft is not a submitted report; the student chooses whether to send it.

## About journey

The About page now begins with one paragraph describing Product Alchemy’s MIS 6393 student-project origins, early research, the First 30 Days Navigator and its successive refinements. The four existing team photos and names follow the paragraph in the same order.

## Privacy and validation

The privacy notice explains the group sites, optional local copies, calendar exports and report drafts. Account deletion on the current site clears that account’s Group B local records there. Browser data on another subdomain or device must be cleared there.

Release validation preserves all earlier versions and the 113 historical entries with their existing owner-confirmation attribution. Tests cover the new arithmetic, origins, account/host storage separation, cleanup, time zones, calendar escaping, optional alerts and arrival-route construction.
